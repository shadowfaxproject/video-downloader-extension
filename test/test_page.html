Sample Video Testing Page

This page hosts a sample HTML5 video to verify that your Main Video Downloader Chrome extension detects the primary video and facilitates direct downloading.

Test 2: HLS Stream (.m3u8 Playlist)

Many modern websites serve video as fragmented HLS (.m3u8) streams instead of single .mp4 files. The extension now automatically downloads and stitches all segments into a playable video file!

How to Test the Extension

  1. Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions.
  2. Enable Developer mode and click Reload (↻) on the extension card.
  3. Open this page (test/test_page.html) in Chrome.
  4. Play either the direct MP4 video or the HLS stream.
  5. Click the extension icon in the toolbar.
    • For direct MP4: Click Download Video.
    • For HLS Stream (.m3u8): Click Download & Assemble Video. You will see a live progress bar as it fetches and stitches the segments into a playable video!
